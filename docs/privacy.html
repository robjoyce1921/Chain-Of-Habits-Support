Overview

Chain of Habits is a habit tracking app focused on daily execution. This policy explains what data the app stores, how it is used, and your choices.

Data We Store

The app stores information needed to provide core functionality, including:

  • Habits you create (identity, title, category, notes, schedule, reminder configuration, icon/color, daily target/unit/rule, ordering, and active status).
  • Goals you create and linked habit relationships.
  • Completion history (date/time, source such as app, widget, sync, or restore, and unit count).
  • Settings (appearance, notification, weekly summary, Last Call, and coaching-copy preferences).
  • Device-local experiment, feedback-recency, and milestone presentation state used by in-app coaching and celebrations.

How Data Is Used

  • To display your habit dashboard, streaks, momentum heatmap, and weekly execution feedback.
  • To schedule local notifications and same-day follow-up reminders you request.
  • To synchronize supported app data across your devices through Apple CloudKit when iCloud is signed in and available.
  • To export and restore your own habit/completion data (CSV) when you choose.

CSV Export and Import

A CSV export contains habits and completion history. It does not contain goals/links; Settings/appearance; experiments/reports; insight snoozes, dismissals, or feedback recency; milestone seen/queue/share presentation state; notification authorization; CloudKit diagnostics; or other device-local records. Pending notifications and completion sync work are derived after import, not restored from CSV.

Import accepts one combined export or exactly one habits CSV plus one completions CSV selected together. The app validates the complete selection, shows a review, and performs a merge-only atomic local update after confirmation. Matching records may be updated and missing records added; unrelated or omitted app records are not deleted. Completion changes remain pending locally and are attempted later when CloudKit sync is enabled and available. Production enables this background sync by default; a successful local import does not confirm upload.

File access is temporary and security scoped to the URLs you select in Apple's document picker. The app copies at most 5 MiB and 100,000 CSV records in aggregate for validation, releases file access on success, error, or cancellation, and does not retain permission to browse the surrounding storage provider. CSV files you export or keep in another app/provider are governed by the privacy and retention choices of that destination.

CloudKit / iCloud Sync

Chain Of Habits saves its working data locally first. The production app enables automatic background CloudKit sync by default. When you are signed in to iCloud and CloudKit is available, supported habit, goal, settings, and completion state can be transmitted to your private iCloud database and synchronized across your devices. The app remains usable when iCloud is signed out, restricted, temporarily unavailable, or offline.

Development and UI-test configurations can explicitly disable CloudKit. CloudKit data handling is governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices. The in-app Cloud Sync Debug view reports the current account and sync status; a local save or CSV import result does not by itself confirm that a cloud upload completed.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The app does not use third-party ad networks.

Data may be transmitted to Apple services for the automatic iCloud/CloudKit sync described above and for standard Apple platform services.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is kept until you delete it.
  • You can use Settings → Clear All Data to remove visible habits, goals, completion history, and reset settings locally.
  • Inactive habit deletion tombstones and completion sync bookkeeping may remain locally to prevent deleted cloud data from returning; Clear All Data is not an immediate physical purge of every row.
  • You can remove the app to delete local data from a device.
  • Cloud data may persist according to Apple CloudKit behavior and your iCloud account state.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
